1. Current conclusion / Conclusión actual

Hof University of Applied Sciences appears to be a realistic and well-aligned first choice for an Informatics Engineering student. The main immediate issue is not learning German from zero; it is documenting the language level that the destination and IPN will accept, and preparing a complete nomination file on the applicable internal timeline.

Best current route

Apply to Hof for English-taught coursework and support the application with an accepted English B2 certificate. The conversation’s reading of the Hof entry was that German A2 would not be required for this English-only route.

Do not treat the A2 issue as resolved until it is confirmed

The initial information received mentioned an A2 German certificate in the first week of August. The later interpretation of the ProMAI annex indicated an English B2 route for Hof. Both the applicable certificate and the real deadline require written confirmation from the mobility coordinator.
